LinkVault Q8 Account Deletion Users can request deletion of their LinkVault Q8 account and associated data by sending an email from the email address linked to their account. Email: support@linkvault.info Subject: Delete My LinkVault Q8 Account Please include the email address associated with your LinkVault Q8 account. When the request is verified, the account and associated personal data, saved links, profile information, and synchronized app data will be permanently deleted. Deletion requests will be processed within 7 days.
